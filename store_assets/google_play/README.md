# Google Play Phone Screenshots

Last updated: August 11, 2026

These are the three phone screenshots submitted to the Google Play default
store listing for the 1.0.28 release:

- `Decide_For_Us_Home_google-play-1242x2208.png`
- `Decide_For_Us_Local_Events_google-play-1242x2208.png`
- `Decide_For_Us_Trip_Planner_google-play-1242x2208.png`

Each image is 1242 by 2208 pixels (9:16). They were cropped from the approved
1242 by 2688 iPhone captures to remove the Apple status bar and App Store
return chrome without changing the app UI or text.

Keep these files as the reproducible source for the current Google Play phone
listing. Feature graphics and tablet screenshots are separate assets and were
not replaced in this update.
